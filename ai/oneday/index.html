The Problem

Rolling turnovers cost more
than developers realize.

A one-month rolling key handover keeps 12–24 construction staff on site, accumulates financing costs on a fully-built asset, and delivers owners a first impression that doesn't match what they paid for. Nobody chooses it. It happens because the turnover was never run as its own project. Our path to success begins with a regimented 200-point QC process that checks, and when necessary re-checks, the entirety of the home.

In Ontario it costs you in three places at once. Miss the Firm Occupancy Date and Tarion pays your purchasers $150 a day per unit. Let occupancy run long and the project carries debt while sale proceeds sit locked until registration. And every week a deficiency stays open is another week a wobbling purchaser has a reason not to close.
